The biggest AI story this week wasn't a model launch. It was a resignation.

1️⃣ Nvidia's CEO Jensen Huang resigned on 12 November, effective immediately. CFO Colette Kress is interim CEO while the board looks at internal and external candidates. Nvidia's filing gives no reason. The shares fell more than 14% at one point that day.
Why it matters: most of the world's AI runs on Nvidia chips. A sudden change at the top of the AI supply chain is something every company buying AI will watch.

2️⃣ TSMC will invest up to US$1 billion in Anthropic and OpenAI, split equally between the two.
Why it matters: the company that manufactures AI chips now wants a stake in the companies that use them.

3️⃣ India released three home-grown AI models under the IndiaAI Mission. EKA-1 from Soket Labs has 26.3 billion parameters and supports all 22 scheduled Indian languages plus English. Dhīr 2B and 5B from ZenteiQ can run offline.
Why it matters: offline, multilingual AI is what government services and low-connectivity India actually need.

The takeaway: AI power is concentrating in a few chip and model companies, while countries like India build options of their own.
