Four stories the world is watching this week.
One. Trump is in Manila for the first Quad leaders' meeting in two years, with Japan, Australia and India. On Thursday, he meets Xi Jinping and Vladimir Putin together in China.
Two. Yemen's government forces pulled back near the Bab al-Mandab strait, a route for twelve percent of world trade. Iran-backed Houthis now hold ground beside it.
Three. A hundred and twenty-eight countries are meeting in Geneva on weapons that choose targets without a human. The US wants non-binding guidelines instead of a treaty.
Four. The EU sanctioned Tatyana Kim, founder of Russia's online marketplace Wildberries. It says the company helps supply the Russian military. Wildberries says it has no business in the EU.
