The man who built the world's most valuable chip company just quit.
Jensen Huang ran Nvidia for thirty-three years.
On November eighth, he told staff he had been unfaithful to his wife. The same day, the board opened an investigation into whether he was the target of a foreign intelligence effort.
The board said it had no reason to believe any Nvidia secrets were compromised.
Four days later, he resigned.
Nvidia shares fell fourteen percent at the open, wiping out about five hundred and forty-six billion dollars, before recovering most of it.
