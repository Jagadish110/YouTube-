Jensen Huang has resigned as CEO of Nvidia. Here is what is known so far.
Nvidia is worth about four and a half trillion dollars, and Huang owns three point six percent of it.
In October, the New York Times reported he was close to an executive at a partner company. U.S. authorities reportedly concluded she was a Chinese intelligence asset. China denies it.
On November eighth, Huang told staff he had not been faithful to his wife, and the board opened an investigation.
The board said it had no reason to believe Nvidia secrets were compromised.
He resigned on November twelfth. Shares fell fourteen percent at the open, and closed down four point three percent.
