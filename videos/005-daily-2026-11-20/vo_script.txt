Ten stories in depth first, then four quick hits. Let's begin.
Story one. The leaders of the United States, China and Russia met together in Shun Jen, China, on Thursday.
It was the first sit down of the three. President Trump met Xi and Putin one on one first, then all three talked for a little over an hour.
They released a four point peace declaration. It covers United Nations reform, oil supply, lowering nuclear risk, and a warning that international courts must not abuse their power.
It did not mention the war in Ukraine, or the war with Iran.
Who is affected? Ukraine did not take part. Nor did Germany, France, Britain, Japan or South Korea.
Why it matters: the United States and Russia each have more than five thousand nuclear warheads, and China has more than six hundred. The leaders said a nuclear war should never be fought, but they announced no cuts.
What happens next? They agreed to start a dialogue on reducing nuclear risks. Mister Trump said he will hold a news conference at the White House on Friday.
Story two. The top United Nations court has ruled that Myanmar committed genocide against the Ro hin ja people.
The International Court of Justice gave its ruling on Thursday, by nine votes to six.
The judges said soldiers, police and border guards killed around ten thousand Ro hin ja civilians in two thousand seventeen.
Hundreds of thousands fled to Bangladesh. About one point two million Ro hin ja still live in camps there.
The case was brought in twenty nineteen by a small country, The Gambia. Myanmar says its operations were a response to attacks by a militant group.
Why it matters: this is the first time this court has found a state responsible for genocide.
What happens next? The court ordered Myanmar to stop ongoing violations, investigate suspects and give restitution. Myanmar has two years to report back.
Story three. The leaders of the Quad met in Manila, in the Philippines, on Tuesday.
The Quad is a group of four countries. Prime Minister Modi, President Trump, Prime Minister Albanese and Prime Minister Ta kai chi took part.
It was their first summit in two years. The last one was in Wilmington, in the United States, in twenty twenty four.
Mister Modi called the Quad a constructive and trusted partnership, covering sea safety, supply chains, new technology and disaster response.
Mister Trump said the group is launching a Quad A I accelerator, with more than eighty five million dollars to help countries in the Indo Pacific use trusted A I.
He also announced deals to secure critical minerals, so that no single supplier can use them as pressure. He did not name China.
What happens next? A first Quad Business Leaders Forum will be held, and more new projects will be announced next year.
Story four. A gas tanker with Indian sailors on board was attacked in the Strait of Hor mooz on Monday, the sixteenth of November.
The ship is called Tara Gas. It carries cooking gas, L P G, and flies the flag of Panama.
Twenty four Indians were on board. Twenty one were taken ashore safely at Khasab, in Oman. Two are injured in hospital. One is missing.
India's foreign ministry condemned the attack. It said attacks on commercial ships must end and free sailing must be restored.
Why it matters: this is the second attack in a week on a tanker with Indian sailors. On the eleventh, another tanker was hit off the United Arab Emirates, and all twenty two crew were rescued.
The strait carried about one fifth of the world's crude oil before the conflict with Iran.
What happens next? The Indian embassy in Oman is working with Omani authorities to search for the missing sailor.
Story five. A big coal company is selling shares to the public for the first time. It is called Mahanadi Coalfields.
An I P O, or initial public offer, is when a company sells its shares to the public so it can be listed on the stock exchange.
Mahanadi is fully owned by Coal India. It runs seventeen mines in Odisha, fourteen open pit and three underground.
The price band is one hundred and twenty nine to one hundred and thirty six rupees a share. The issue is worth about eight thousand nine hundred and eighty one crore rupees. A crore is ten million.
All the shares are being sold by Coal India. So the company itself gets no money from this sale.
In the last financial year, the company's profit after tax was about ten thousand six hundred and ninety eight crore rupees, down one percent.
Bidding closes on the twenty third of November. Listing is expected on the twenty seventh. This is not investment advice.
Story six. Cyclone Ack van is heading for the coast of Andhra Pradesh. The weather department expects it to cross the coast on Friday, the twentieth, around noon.
It is expected to cross between Nell ore and Too nee, as a very severe cyclonic storm.
Winds of one hundred and twenty to one hundred and thirty kilometres an hour are expected, with gusts up to one hundred and forty five.
Extremely heavy rain, more than twenty one centimetres in a day, is forecast for north coastal Andhra on the nineteenth and twentieth.
Who is affected? Coastal Andhra Pradesh, south Odisha and Tamil Nadu. Fishermen have been asked not to go to sea, and Odisha has begun moving people from vulnerable areas.
Why it matters: this is the third cyclone in under a month. Andhra Pradesh says two earlier storms have already caused losses of more than seven thousand crore rupees.
What happens next? Officials will track the storm as it crosses the coast. Rain is also forecast for Odisha on the twenty first.
Story seven. Uttar Pradesh has published its draft list of voters, ahead of the state assembly election in twenty twenty seven.
The list has thirteen point five eight crore voters. That is about eighteen point eight four lakh more than in April. A lakh is one hundred thousand.
There are seven point three eight crore men, six point two zero crore women, and four thousand and forty two voters of the third gender.
Eleven point eight three lakh voters are aged eighteen to nineteen.
Why it matters: the election officer says about sixteen crore people in the state are eighteen or older, based on population estimates. So the list is about two point five eight crore short of that estimate.
What happens next? People can file claims and objections from the seventeenth of November to the sixteenth of December. Special camps run on the twenty first, twenty second, twenty eighth and twenty ninth of November. The final list comes out on the fourth of January.
Story eight. Ladakh will vote on the fourth of December to elect its hill development councils.
Newly set up councils will cover all eight districts of Ladakh, with one hundred seats in all.
Thirty six of the hundred seats are reserved for women, for the first time.
Nominations closed on the seventeenth. Today, the twentieth, is the last day to withdraw.
Not everyone agrees. Earlier this month Kargil saw a full shutdown, called by the Kargil Democratic Alliance, over timing and how seats were drawn. In Leh, the Apex Body asked to delay the vote to April or May because of winter snow.
What happens next? Voting is on the fourth of December, and the whole process is to finish by the eighth.
Story nine. In Maharashtra, farmers hit by drought are getting an advance payment of five thousand rupees each.
Chief Minister Devendra Fadnavis announced it on the fourteenth of October, before Diwali. Drought has been declared in around three hundred talukas.
The government estimated the cost at six thousand crore rupees, reaching about one crore ten lakh to one crore twenty lakh farmers.
Mister Fadnavis called it unprecedented, and said it cannot cover all losses. Each eligible farmer gets at least five thousand rupees now, and the rest is paid later.
Opposition parties and farmers have criticised the amount as too small. A loan waiver for another three and a half lakh farmers is also to begin.
What happens next? The reports we checked, from October, do not say whether every farmer has been paid yet.
Story ten. The Union government has approved more than three hundred and fifty crore rupees for farming in Punjab.
Agriculture Minister Shivraj Singh Chouhan handed over the approval letters at Badarpur village, in Mo ha lee district, on Tuesday, the seventeenth.
Two hundred and fifty four point two zero crore rupees is for this year under a scheme called P M R K V Y. Ninety eight point four four crore is for seven new projects.
The event was part of the Khet Bachao Abhiyan, or Save the Field campaign. Mister Chouhan spoke about crop residue and new farming technology.
Green ammonia, made with solar power, is being tried in place of urea on ten thousand hectares across the country, including one thousand and eight hectares in Punjab.
Mister Chouhan said the only aim is to improve the lives of the farmers of Punjab.
Quick hit one. Voyager One, launched in nineteen seventy seven, reached one light day from Earth on the eighteenth of November. A radio signal now takes a full day to reach it.
It is about twenty five point nine billion kilometres away, the farthest any human made object has gone.
Quick hit two. Amazon says it will invest seven billion dollars more in renewable energy in India by two thousand thirty, on top of four billion dollars already contracted.
Four new projects add up to nine hundred and thirty four megawatts, in Karnataka, Madhya Pradesh and Rajasthan.
Quick hit three. South Korea's top court on Thursday overturned the conviction of former prime minister Han Dook soo for helping with the martial law order of December twenty twenty four.
His fifteen year sentence was overturned, and the case now goes back to the Seoul High Court.
Quick hit four. Fiji has declared a nationwide state of disaster because of a drought linked to El Nino. It lasts up to thirty days and can be extended.
More than two million litres of emergency water were delivered to island communities in the past month.
