# voice.md — Trendsplain (Jagadish's faceless channel: whatever's trending, explained; core: Indian business)

Inferred from the scripts of videos 001 (Zepto), 002 (Blinkit) and 003 (Coca-Cola IPO). setup.sh copies
this file to ~/.claude/youtube/voice.md so the yt-* skills find it. Edit this copy in the repo, not that one.

## Who I am talking to

One person: a 20-to-35-year-old Indian who uses Blinkit, Zepto or Zomato every week, has heard the
headlines ("unicorn", "IPO", "₹ crore losses") and wants to actually understand how the business works
in four minutes, without jargon or hype. Possibly a student, a first job in tech or finance, or someone
thinking about starting up.

## How I actually talk

The voice is a calm, curious explainer (the AI voice "Eric"), never a hype host. Short spoken sentences,
one idea per line. Numbers are said in full and rounded honestly ("almost twenty-one thousand crore").
Typical moves:
- Open on the surprising number or contradiction, then ask the question the video answers:
  "Blinkit took almost twenty-one thousand crore rupees in orders in just three months. And for the first
  time, it's actually making money doing it. So how does a ten-minute delivery app turn a profit?"
- Signpost: "Here's the first thing to understand…", "The second thing…", "Here's the scorecard."
- Explain a term the first time in plain words ("A dark store is a small warehouse inside your neighbourhood.").
- Name the tension near the end ("So what's the catch?") and give both outcomes.
- End with one question for the comments, then "Subscribe" plus the next video's topic.

## Words I never use

"Game-changer", "insane", "mind-blowing", "unlock", "dive in", "let's get into it", "smash that like button",
"guys", "crazy", "you won't believe". No "buy", "sell", "target price" or "multibagger" in any form.

## Words I do use

"Here's the thing", "So what's the catch?", "That's the real signal", "Let's break it down", "crore", "lakh",
"adjusted", "on paper", Indian place and brand names exactly as Indians say them.

## What I will not claim

- Any number without a source that was opened; every number appears with a source line on screen.
- Investment advice. IPO videos say and show "this is not investment advice".
- Private life, rumours or unproven allegations about founders. Public facts only.
- Results for the channel itself (views, subscribers) unless Jagadish gives them.

## My format

- Faceless motion graphics with drawn illustrations; each video in its company's brand colours.
- 2.5 to 4 minutes, about 150 words a minute, no intro sequence, no sponsor read.
- Synthesised sound effects (typing, ticks, pops, whooshes); no background song.
- Uploads Mon / Wed / Fri. Shorts are cut from the long videos.
- English with Indian terms (crore, lakh); names respelled in the script so the voice says them right.
