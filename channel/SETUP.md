# Trendsplain — channel setup kit

**Name:** Trendsplain · **Handle:** @Trendsplain (pick @TrendsplainIN or @Trendsplain_ if it is taken)
**Tagline:** Whatever's trending, explained.
**Brand colours:** navy #111632, yellow #FFD23F, coral #FF5A5F, sky #5CC8FF · **Fonts:** Outfit (headline), Plus Jakarta Sans

Files in this folder:
- `profile.png`: 800×800 profile picture (YouTube shows it as a circle; the arrow stays inside)
- `banner.png`: 2560×1440 banner; the logo and name sit inside the 1546×423 area every device shows
- `logo.svg`: logo for watermarks, end cards and anything else

## Create the channel (about 10 minutes, on your phone or laptop)

1. Sign in to YouTube with the Google account you want to own it. Use a separate brand account so you can add helpers later:
   youtube.com → profile picture → Settings → "Add or manage your channel(s)" → **Create a channel** → enter **Trendsplain**.
2. Customise channel → **Handle**: `@Trendsplain`.
3. **Branding:** upload `profile.png` as the picture and `banner.png` as the banner. Video watermark: upload `profile.png`, show it for the entire video.
4. **Basic info:** paste the description below, and add links (Instagram, and later the website).
5. Settings → Channel → **Country: India**; Keywords: paste the keyword line below.
6. Verify the channel with your phone number (youtube.com/verify) so you can upload custom thumbnails and videos over 15 minutes.
